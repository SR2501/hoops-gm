| R60 | **A test can be accurate and non-independent, and only accuracy is usually checked** | 🔴 | A reviewer moved all three fixture PDFs out of the tree on 2026-08-21 and the injury lane's central vocabulary test still passed, because it asserted against `status_counts` in a JSON file **the same lane had written** — and every recorded count was correct, which is exactly what made it dangerous. Any assertion whose expected value derives from an artefact produced by the code under test is a tautology wearing a measurement costume, and it passes hardest when the recorded value happens to be right. Remedy is the technique that found it: **move or delete the source of truth and see whether the test notices.** Distinct from R50, which is a verifier never seen failing — this one can fail; it is measuring a copy of itself | `data-engineer` |
| R61 | **An artefact that looks more like proof is not thereby covering more** | 🟡 | Replacing a crude leak filter with something principled took its checked cells from 131 to 60, and the 75 lost were the numeric rates — which for a real Basketball Monster export **are the paid content the guard exists to keep off stdout**. Remedy: **state what a guard inspects and what it protects as two sentences, neither inferred from the other**, and lead with the item whose mechanism covers most of the claim rather than the item that looks most like proof. | R62 | **A guard that cries wolf is the guard the next person loosens, and they will loosen it toward vacuous** | 🟡 | An ADR-002 detector concatenated a rendered subtree's `textContent` and searched for the forbidden product as a string; it passed on a one-row synthetic payload and reported **over 200 violations against the real 60-row cohort, every one false**, because a table's `textContent` runs adjacent cells together, so `12.34` beside `5.67` contains `345`.
