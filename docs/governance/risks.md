Mitigation: state what each check can and cannot observe at the point it is written; pair it with one that sees what it structurally cannot (line-span containment beside AST comparison, driving a real condition beside reading its copy, establishing *why* a fingerprint moved beside observing *that* it did); and **expect the check to fire, and expect not to know why yet** | `architect` |
| R55 | **A placeholder pair is internally consistent, so cross-field agreement cannot detect a fabricated value** | 🔴 | Found 2026-08-21. In `nba_scheduleleaguev2_2026_27_pending_knockout.json` this is not an edge case: **all 18 resolved games carry year 1900 in `gameTimeEst`**, and the 6 pending games carry year 0001. | R57 | **A check that iterates over recorded entries cannot see a deleted entry** | 🔴 | Found 2026-08-21, mutation-proven by the position lane. `test_every_recorded_source_fingerprint_matches_the_file_today` iterates `manifest["operator"]["source_fingerprints"].items()` and compares each recorded digest to the file today. It therefore validates **the values of the keys that are present** and says nothing about which files ought to be present; its only membership assertion is that the mapping is non-empty. Mutation: deleting a watched file's key leaves the suite **green**; corrupting a retained key's value turns it **red**.
